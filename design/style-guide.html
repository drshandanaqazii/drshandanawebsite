Colour

White, ink, and one colour.

Amethyst is the only hue on the page at all; gilt marks, and never fills. Everything else is white, ink or a hairline. No red, no alert colour anywhere in the marketing surface — and no ambient wash behind any of it.

The panel

The only gradient left on the site.

The portrait is a cut-out, so the panel behind it is the one surface that cannot be white or the coat disappears. White at the head, the faintest amethyst at the foot where the coat is. Nothing else on the site is graded — never type, buttons or cards.

#FFFFFF  →  #F5F4F8  →  #E7E0F4

Typography

One family. Weight does the work.

There is no serif here. Outfit sets every display line, light at the top of the scale and heavier as the sizes fall, so one family reads as two volumes of the same voice. Inter carries everything functional. Emphasis is amethyst, never a slant.

The signature move

One word per line goes italic.

It is the entire personality of this system in a single gesture, and the fastest way to lose it is to use it twice in one headline. One word. Always the emotional one — never the noun that carries the information.

Notes

This sheet is intentionally single-theme. A white-page palette can only be judged on its own ground, so it renders light for every viewer. The site built from it will be single-theme for the same reason.

Tokens live in design/tokens.css; the reasoning lives in design/design-system.md. Bracketed values — state, years in practice — are placeholders awaiting real details.
